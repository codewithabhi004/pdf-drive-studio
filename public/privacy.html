Privacy Policy

PDF Drive Studio · Last updated November 23, 2026

PDF Drive Studio lets you read, edit, create, and convert PDF documents in your browser. This policy explains what happens to files and account information when you use the app.

Files and documents

PDFs and images are processed in your browser and are not sent to a PDF Drive Studio server. If you sign in to Google, generated outputs are uploaded to your Google Drive folder named PDF_Drive_Studio. You can browse and download files in that folder from the app. Google processes those uploads under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

Google account information

If you choose Google sign-in, the app receives your name, email address, and profile picture to show your signed-in account. It requests access to create and manage files made available to this app in Google Drive. The access token is kept in browser memory for the active session and is not saved by PDF Drive Studio to a server.

Storage and sharing

PDF Drive Studio does not operate a server-side document store and does not sell personal information. Files uploaded through Google Drive remain in your Google account and are managed by you. You can sign out of the app and revoke its Google access from your Google Account security settings.
